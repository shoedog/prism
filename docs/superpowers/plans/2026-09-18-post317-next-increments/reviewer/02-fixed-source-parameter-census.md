## Scope discipline

The observer may measure; it may not repair native ownership/slots/entries,
admit production syntax, add dependencies, use a compiler Program, infer
runtime edges or generalize beyond this package. A nonzero cohort recommends
at most one later spec and grants no implementation authority. An unavailable
or incomplete packet is `input_blocked`/`incomplete`, never zero demand.

Report WRONG items first, then SMELL items, exact evidence hashes and one final
verdict: `APPROVE`, `REVISE`, or `PARK`. At the round cap, closed finite defects
may receive one disclosed targeted batch; recurring open-class failures park
for design.
